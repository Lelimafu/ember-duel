Ember Duel — Asha, the Ember Mage vs Bram, the Tidecaller. Both start at 30 HP. Each round play one card: Ember beats Thorn, Thorn beats Tide, Tide beats Ember — element advantage wins no matter the power; same element, higher power wins; exact tie is a clash and both draw. The winner deals damage equal to its power. Heal restores +4 HP instead of dealing damage, Shield blocks the next damage, Surge doubles power this round. First to 0 HP loses. Controls: click a card or press 1–5 to play, Enter start / next, R restart, M mute. Staged screenshots: ?shot=title|play|win|loss&seed=1.
